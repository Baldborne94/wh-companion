WH Companion

A free companion app for Warhammer hobbyists. Read your ebooks, follow the reading orders, track your painting, browse the lore and listen to ambient music — all in one place, synced to your Google account across your devices.

Open the app →

What you can do

  • Library & reader — read EPUB and PDF ebooks with bookmarks, highlights and saved progress.
  • Reading order — curated guides for the Horus Heresy and Age of Sigmar's Path to Glory.
  • Painting tracker — track each miniature's steps, with an optional AI colour-scheme advisor.
  • Lore — tap keywords while reading to jump straight to the wiki.
  • Music — play your own YouTube playlists as ambient background while you read or paint.
  • Sync — sign in with Google so your library and progress follow you everywhere.

Sign in

WH Companion uses Google sign-in to create your account and keep your data in sync. If you connect YouTube, the app requests read-only access purely to show your own playlists inside the app — it never modifies your account. See how your data is handled in our Privacy Policy.
