Privacy Policy

WH Companion · Last updated: 29 June 2026

WH Companion ("the App") is a personal companion web app for Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar hobbyists. It lets you read ebooks, track reading progress and painting, browse lore, and listen to ambient music. This page explains what data the App handles and why. The App is operated by an individual developer, reachable at loprestialessandro94@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information to create and identify your account.
  • App content you create. Reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, painting-tracker steps, and ebook files you upload are stored so the App can sync them across your devices.
  • YouTube data. If you connect YouTube, the App uses the YouTube Data API (read-only) to display your playlists and search results. This data is shown to you in the App only; it is not stored on our servers or shared with anyone.
  • Painting photos (optional). If you use the AI Color Advisor, the miniature photos you upload are sent to an AI provider (Anthropic) to generate colour suggestions. Photos are not used for any other purpose.

2. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and keep your library, progress and settings in sync.
  • To provide the features you actively use (reading, painting tracker, music, AI advisor).
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The App requests read-only access and never modifies your YouTube account. You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

4. Where your data is stored

Account data and app content are stored using Supabase (database, authentication and file storage). Uploaded ebook files are kept in a private storage bucket accessible only to your account. AI Color Advisor requests are processed by Anthropic.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can disconnect YouTube at any time from within the App, and you can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing loprestialessandro94@gmail.com. We will delete it within a reasonable time after your request.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: loprestialessandro94@gmail.com
